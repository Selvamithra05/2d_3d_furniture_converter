Diffusion Model Explanation
Shap-E uses a diffusion probabilistic model:
Process:
Start with random noise

Gradually denoise step-by-step

Learn structure from training data

Produce meaningful 3D representation


Limitations

❌ May generate imperfect geometry
❌ Texture quality is limited
❌ Requires GPU for faster performance
❌ Not fully accurate for complex shapes
